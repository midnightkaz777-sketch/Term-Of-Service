Privacy Policy

DevKaz Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how DevKaz Bot may collect, process, and use information when you interact with the bot or use a Discord server where the bot is installed.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled by a server, DevKaz Bot may process information such as:

  • Discord user ID
  • Discord username and display name
  • Server membership information
  • Roles assigned to a member
  • Messages involved in configured logging systems
  • Ticket and application submissions
  • Verification activity
  • Security and moderation events

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide and operate DevKaz Bot's features, including:

  • Server verification
  • Ticket and application management
  • Security and moderation
  • Server logging
  • Member management
  • Server statistics
  • YouTube notifications

4. Server Administrators

Server administrators control which DevKaz Bot features are enabled on their server.

Administrators may also control access to tickets, applications, logs, and other server-management information.

5. Tickets & Applications

Information submitted through tickets or staff applications may be visible to authorized server staff members.

Do not submit passwords, account tokens, payment information, or other highly sensitive information through the bot.

6. Logging

When logging features are enabled, the bot may record events such as member joins, member departures, message edits, message deletions, role changes, channel changes, moderation events, verification events, and security events.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as it is needed for server administration, moderation, security, or the operation of the bot's features.

Retention may vary depending on the configuration of the individual Discord server.

8. Data Sharing

DevKaz Bot does not intentionally sell personal information.

Information processed by the bot may be accessible to authorized server administrators or staff when required for the bot's configured features.

9. Discord

DevKaz Bot operates through Discord. Discord may independently collect and process information according to its own policies.

10. Security

Reasonable measures are used to help protect information handled by DevKaz Bot. However, no online service can guarantee complete security.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when DevKaz Bot's features or data practices change.

The updated version will be published on this page.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DevKaz Bot, contact the DevKaz Community staff through the official Discord server.

13. Agreement

By using DevKaz Bot, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy and understand how the bot may process information.
